Playbook

How I run a creator program

The same five stages, whether it's 10 creators or 100, in any market.

  1. Brief and goal

    Turn the brand ask into one measurable goal, an audience and a budget per creator tier.

    ObjectiveBudget

  2. Creator selection

    Shortlist on audience fit, past content and engagement quality, not follower count alone.

    FitER

  3. Negotiation

    Agree rates, deliverables, usage rights and timelines that protect both sides.

    RateRights

  4. Content and approvals

    Brief creators, manage drafts and brand approvals, keep every post on schedule.

    On-time

  5. Report and optimise

    Report what moved, then reallocate budget to the creators and formats that worked.

    ReachCPRConv.

What I plan for in European markets

Ad disclosure rules
Paid posts need clear labelling, and the rules differ by country. In Germany that means "Werbung" or "Anzeige".
Creator data under GDPR
Creator contact and performance data is personal data. It needs a lawful basis and careful storage.
Many markets, many languages
DACH, the Nordics and Southern Europe need their own creators, not one translated brief.
