OpenAI just shipped Dots: agents that work between your chats.
Claude and Grok are in the same race.

Every one of them promises "it does it for you."

Here's my filter for client work:
→ Reports, reminders, status: agent drafts
→ Spikes and anomalies: agent flags
→ Bad news, pricing, renewals: you
→ Anything that leaves the building: a human reads it first

The bad-news test:
If you'd phone the client, don't let an agent write it.
If you'd send the same note to 40 clients, automate it.

I'd rather be the account manager who had the answer at 6am than the one who heard about it at 9:02.
