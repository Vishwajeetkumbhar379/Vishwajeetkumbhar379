Your AI ad judge has a favourite.
It's not your best ad.

Models that rank copy lean on habits:
→ the option shown first
→ the longer option
→ their own writing
→ one vague overall score

The fix stack:
→ Yes/no rubric, not one score
→ Swap the order, run it twice
→ A different model as judge
→ Check it against ads with known results

I'd rather spend an hour testing the judge than a month running ads it picked.

Source: Zheng et al. 2023, LLM-as-a-Judge (MT-Bench).
